On winning The Quietly Crushing It Award

In May, I was honoured to receive The Quietly Crushing It Award at the fourth annual Manitoba Tech Awards, hosted by Tech Thursday and Manitoba Innovates. The award is for "someone doing consistently excellent work without needing a spotlight, a drumroll, or a LinkedIn essay about it" — so I'll keep this short.

Most of my work at Chekkit happens behind the scenes: building product, shipping features, and helping our team support the businesses that rely on us every day. It's not always glamorous, but it's the part of building a company I enjoy most, and it was really meaningful to have that kind of work recognized.

Thank you to Tech Thursday, Manitoba Innovates, and everyone who makes the Manitoba tech community what it is. It was a great night, and I'm proud to be building here.
